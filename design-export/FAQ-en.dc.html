FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is li-fe.ai?

li-fe.ai is your digital life companion in live conversation. You talk to Lisa or Felix about what’s on your mind – for more clarity, direction and the confidence to act.

Are Lisa and Felix real people?

No. Lisa and Felix are digital, AI-powered conversation partners. They were developed by occupational therapists and psychotherapists with professional experience in therapy, counselling and life skills.

Is it a chat?

No. At li-fe.ai you speak directly with Lisa or Felix – just like a face-to-face conversation. You talk, they listen, ask the right questions and work out concrete next steps with you.

Does li-fe.ai replace therapy?

No. li-fe.ai does not replace psychotherapy or medical treatment and does not constitute either. In an acute crisis, please contact the Telefonseelsorge crisis line in Germany (0800 111 0 111) or emergency services on 112.

How much does li-fe.ai cost?

The first 5 minutes are free. After that you choose the plan that suits you. You can find all details under Pricing.

When can I talk to Lisa or Felix?

Anytime. Lisa and Felix are there for you around the clock (24/7).

What happens to my data?

li-fe.ai is GDPR compliant. Your data is processed in the EU to the highest data protection standards. Your conversations stay confidential and are not passed on to third parties.
